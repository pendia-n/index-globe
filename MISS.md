# Teder release gap audit

23 November 2026 · Read-only audit of the [live Worker](https://teder.pendia-community.workers.dev/) and application commit `b1a549a`. This document records findings and proposed fixes.

**Verdict: the deployment is up and the narrow two-company workflow works in an isolated Worker. It is not a complete trading website and is not ready for a real pilot.** I shipped a plain-text brand and a placeholder hero even though the SVG was available and the [implementation plan](implementation.md#user-experience-and-screens) called for it. I also described the local workflow as a finished v1 without making the missing screens, manual evidence, and production activation gap clear enough.

## What I checked

- **Live UI:** I opened the landing page, followed the registration link, inspected the username-first recovery page, and opened the workspace as a guest and followed its sign-in link. The user's mobile screenshot shows the same registration structure. I inspected the public page and asset responses separately. The production walkthrough stopped at sign-in.
- **Deployed infrastructure:** Wrangler showed a full deployment of Worker version `6787c31f-935b-42db-bd8f-11148ccd3bcf` after the signing secret was added. Read-only aggregate counts from production D1 returned **0 reviewers, 0 companies, 0 approved companies, 0 requests, 0 deals and 0 settlements** at audit time. No accounts, approvals, trades, or writes were created in production for this audit.
- **Backend:** I repeated the test against a copy of the built Worker with a fresh local D1 database and separate test-only signing material. Registration, company approval, a staff invitation, private requests, quotes, a counteroffer, offer selection, both agreement acknowledgments, scheduling, a shortfall dispute, corrected delivery, the external settlement reference, and recovery and logout all passed. This used fictional records and a controlled delivery window. It establishes that those actions execute and persist. It does not establish contract execution, real meter data, partner access, or movement of money or electricity.

## Brand and interface

**The SVG is sitting at the project root as `teder.svg`.** I verified it exists, is square (230 × 230), and is about 812 KB. It was never connected to the header or copied into the served assets. The root layout declares a stylesheet and title but no icon. Both `/favicon.ico` and `/teder.svg` return a 200 HTML page saying “Page not found”. The old scaffold's `/drizzle.svg` actually returns an SVG. A success-status check alone missed this. [Header markup](chamin/src/teder/app.tsx#L33) · [Document head](chamin/src/routes/__root.tsx#L6)

**The landing page and several promised routes are too thin.** The four-hour diagram is a coded sample, with a low-contrast “Illustrative terms” label. The CSS colors for that label and its panel calculate to **2.07:1**, below the 4.5:1 target for normal text. The linked signup is real and the forms have labels. The site still reads as a scaffold because its primary experience is a slogan, a static sample and a form. About, Pricing and Announcements show the same 200 not-found shell. Unknown routes should return a real 404. [Landing markup](chamin/src/teder/app.tsx#L8) · [Styles](chamin/src/styles.css) · [WCAG contrast criterion](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/contrast-minimum.html)

**Design direction for the rebuild:** Use the provided SVG in the header, authentication, and workspace. Preserve the original artwork, optimize a faithful web version, and derive a 16/32 px `favicon.ico` from the same mark. Supply an SVG favicon and an appropriate touch icon. Check light and dark tabs at actual size. Remove the unrelated scaffold icon. An image response with the correct content type, and a visible browser tab icon, are both release checks.

Use **navy blue `#142235`** as the main brand color, **earth brown `#705343`** for secondary accents, **ember red `#B83221`** for primary actions, and neutral stone surfaces. Reserve readable, labeled status treatments for verified, pending and exception states so a red button never carries trade status by itself. Present **solar, wind, hydro, nuclear, coal and gas** as source labels with clear availability states. Electricity generated from gas or coal is a separate offering from physical gas or coal shipments. The homepage should explain the scope using real or deliberately commissioned energy infrastructure imagery, a readable **example** that shows a request becoming a firm offer and delivery record, and clear links to market coverage and signup. Show the actual pilot separately from planned products. Avoid invented activity, prices, counterparties or volumes.

## Company signup and seat limits

**What the current app does:** The first registration makes a workspace and one owner. The database makes the trimmed, uppercased registration string globally unique. The legal company name is not the unique key. Additional people can join with single-use, 24-hour invitations as trader, operator or viewer. **There is no five-seat limit** and no staff roster, invite revocation, member removal, or ownership transfer in the site. A company stuck in review gets generic guidance and sees no way to provide evidence or resolve a duplicate claim. [Company and invitation schema](chamin/migrations/0001_teder.sql) · [Registration](chamin/src/teder/api.ts#L62) · [Team screen](chamin/src/teder/workspace.tsx#L59)

**Recommendation: one workspace per legal entity, up to five named users for the pilot, including the owner.** Five is a proposed pilot limit, not a claim about the current release. Larger teams can request more seats. Confirm claims to a company before granting authority. Prevent a pending claim from indefinitely blocking the real representative. If the company already has a workspace, offer a private request to join or a way to contact support. Do not disclose who claimed the company. Show available seats, count active members and outstanding seat reservations consistently, free expired or revoked invitations, and enforce the limit server-side even when invitations are accepted simultaneously. Add role changes, removal, invite revocation, and verified ownership transfer. Each human keeps their own credentials and recovery code.

**Add the country or territory selector before the legal identifier.** State or province is required when it identifies the issuing registry. Store the authority, identifier type, and canonical value as text with leading zeroes and prefixes preserved. Deduplicate by **issuing registry plus identifier type and canonical value**. Keep country of registration separate from the markets the company is permitted to trade in. Check identifier format while the user types. A good format is only the first step. A reviewer should verify the registry record, representative's authority, and separate market eligibility.

| Initial registration option | Label or rule |
| --- | --- |

| Mainland China | **Unified Social Credit Code**, 18 characters. Check the enterprise registry. [Shanghai government registry guidance](https://english.shanghai.gov.cn/en-Business-MarketEntryAccess-MarketAccess/20260818/89a3a21cce3548cea0ec421f4e236575.html) · [Official code structure](https://shanghai.chinatax.gov.cn/gate/big5/shanghai.chinatax.gov.cn/zcfw/rdwd/201510/t419764.shtml) |
| United States | **State of formation and state-issued entity/file number** for a state-registered company. Format varies by registry and entity type. An EIN is a **federal tax ID**, so keep it out of routine signup. [IRS on EIN and state formation](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/employer-identification-number) · [California registry example](https://www.sos.ca.gov/business-programs/business-entities/cbs-search-tips) |

Offer **Other jurisdiction / request review** until more registries have a documented rule set. Accepting a registration inquiry from a country must never make that country a live trading market. Preserve the legal-name field the user liked. Do not guess missing jurisdictions for legacy records during migration.

| P0 · Pilot access | A reviewer flag and manual Approve/Decline screen. | Production has no reviewer, so a new company cannot progress to its first trade unless a trusted reviewer is bootstrapped. Review is a single status and note. There is no structured evidence, eligibility per market, assigned case, reason surfaced to the applicant, or resubmission flow. [Review screen](chamin/src/teder/workspace.tsx#L69) · [Reviewer setup](APP.md#operator-setup) |
| P0 · Request and comparison | An approved buyer can open a real private request for one named supplier. That supplier can quote and counteroffers are versioned. | The buyer has to know the supplier owner's username. The request stores exactly one seller, so it cannot solicit competing suppliers on one requirement or compare their terms. No eligible-counterparty picker, tender invitation list, drafts, withdraw/reopen controls or comparison screen. [Request schema](chamin/migrations/0001_teder.sql#L33) · [Request screen](chamin/src/teder/workspace.tsx#L24) · [Offer UI](chamin/src/teder/workspace.tsx#L87) |
| P0 · Firm offer and contract | Latest unexpired firm offer can be selected once. Both company owners can acknowledge the same agreement reference. | “Firm” is chosen by the supplier. There is no structured support for capacity, route, credit, or terms checks, and no evidence-backed verification badge. “Contracted” comes from matching external agreement references acknowledged by the two owners. There is no signed document in the app, independent signing verification, conditions checklist, amendment or termination workflow. Keep external signing, but let authorized users attach and review its evidence. [Offer and award logic](chamin/src/teder/api.ts#L180) · [Agreement and schedule logic](chamin/src/teder/api.ts#L216) |
| P0 · Delivery and quantity | Supplier enters source, reference and total MWh. Buyer compares the total, raises a dispute and can review a corrected report. | No verified partner account, evidence attachment, trusted feed, interval-by-interval checks, or scheduled quantity. Equal whole-window totals can hide a missed hour. The UI should say **Supplier reported** until the evidence is checked. Store scheduled and delivered quantities by interval, and link the original and corrected evidence. Require an explicit reason when a discrepancy is accepted under the agreement. [Delivery UI](chamin/src/teder/workspace.tsx#L140) · [Delivery records](chamin/src/teder/api.ts#L229) |
| P1 · Coverage, storage and markets | One flat request/deal list and one constant-power time window. | There is no portfolio coverage view, allocation by interval, storage availability, committed capacity, fuel inventory, configurable market, or cross-border route status. Power uses Singapore time, SGD, a free-text delivery point, and no structured energy-source field. The UI cannot distinguish a planned market from one ready for execution. [Workspace data and time handling](chamin/src/teder/client.ts#L14) · [Coverage and storage design](ux.md#2-buyer-workspace-and-coverage) |
| P1 · Records and next actions | JSON download, deal activity, settlement reference and screen-level errors. | No private file vault, document provenance, clear next actor and due time, notifications, search, filters, pagination or useful human-readable deal summary. Settlement reference records what the buyer entered. It is not a payment receipt. Payment checkout, escrow and billing for the energy remain outside scope. If Teder later collects a service fee, design that separately. [Deal workspace](chamin/src/teder/workspace.tsx#L127) |

The pilot uses a single custom server API and D1 SQL. The earlier plan called for oRPC, Drizzle and Better Auth. I changed the implementation approach without documenting the decision clearly. The Worker-specific session and recovery behavior exists. Before exposing sensitive business data, record and review that authentication decision, define access removal and audit retention, and document how to rotate the signing material without making passwords and recovery codes unusable. The test in this report is a functional audit, not a comprehensive security or accessibility assessment.

## Completion gates for the next release

1. **Brand:** real supplied logo renders on public and authenticated pages. Both SVG and ICO requests return image bytes with valid content types. Check the tab icon and the mobile layout. The static hero is replaced and all illustrative content is unmistakable.
2. **Onboarding:** new company, join existing company, pending review and correction paths are distinct. Test two registries that issue the same numeric identifier, leading zeroes, a UK prefixed identifier, a same-company duplicate, simultaneous seat claims, expired and revoked invites, and the recovery-code-only reset.
3. **Trading:** on staging, open a realistic request with two eligible suppliers. Verify private offers, actionable next steps, side-by-side terms, a clear agreement record, and one award. Show accepted terms and the specific role that can advance the deal. Exercise desktop and mobile UI using separate buyer, seller and reviewer accounts.
4. **Evidence:** distinguish estimated, contracted, scheduled, partner-reported, reconciled, and settled quantities. Use a documented example with a missed interval even where totals look similar. Check document privacy, shortfall handling, correction history, usable export and handoff to the real scheduler.
5. **Operations:** provision a trusted reviewer, make review and support visible on the live site, verify deployment and the intended domain, and confirm the pilot's market and delivery arrangements before accepting live firm offers. Report local test, live hosting, staging browser workflow, and real transaction as four separate milestones.

The immediate decision is **one company with five named users, country-aware registration, and a market-specific trading gate**. The immediate build gap is a branded website and a usable tender, agreement and delivery UI over the limited workflow that already exists.
