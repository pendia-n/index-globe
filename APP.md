# Teder

The global energy workspace at `/workspace/energy` accepts private buyer requirements and available supplier supply for natural gas/LNG, crude/refined products, physical coal, electricity from any generation source, and storage/flexibility. The homepage provides both entry points. Delivery jurisdiction uses the existing full country/territory list. The original bunker and Singapore power records remain accessible.

Customers can submit before company approval. A dedicated operator assigns the case, sets a response deadline, and records route clearance after company approval. Supply availability becomes eligible for same-product, same-country suggestions until its expiry. Suggestions require confirmation of delivery point, quantities, timing and specifications. Buyers or operations invite approved suppliers privately. Each supplier sees its own offers.

A buyer selects a current quote version. Operations records the contract reference, payment-security arrangement and fee terms. Both parties accept before supplier delivery reporting. The buyer reviews delivery, supplier records the invoice, buyer reports payment, supplier confirms receipt, and operations reconciles the separately agreed Teder fee. Exceptions pause progress and require a documented resolution. Recorded external evidence references support these checks.

Run `./node_modules/.bin/tsx tests/energy.test.ts`, `./node_modules/.bin/tsc --noEmit`, and `npm run build`. The regression exercises private quote isolation, stale quotes, role gates, exceptions, delivery and fee reconciliation against all migrations in isolated SQLite. Live anonymous page and API checks are separate from real-party transactions. Energy notices feed the existing authenticated inbox. Customer communication stays in-app; no email alerts are sent.

Company owners register one workspace per legal entity using its registration jurisdiction and registry identifier. A reviewer checks the registry record, representative and pilot eligibility before enabling requests and offers. Each company has five named seats, including the owner. An unused invitation reserves a seat for 24 hours or until the owner revokes it. Review authority is granted to a trusted account through an authenticated D1 administrative operation after identity verification. There is no public reviewer registration.

The browser signs in using an HttpOnly signed session cookie. Save the generated recovery code during registration. A password reset requires the username and current recovery code, replaces it with a newly saved code and revokes existing sessions. There is no email reset. The JWT signing secret is stored only in the ignored local environment file and a Worker secret. Keep a protected backup of the signing secret. Rotation requires a deliberate migration because it also protects password and recovery verifiers.

The buyer and seller sign and settle outside Teder. A delivery report identifies its source and evidence reference. Suppliers can include half-hour readings for the buyer to compare against the scheduled amount. The buyer can reconcile delivery or open a dispute and review a corrected report. The app does not move funds, schedule a grid transaction, or independently confirm a meter reading. A live deal requires eligible participants, an appropriate route and review of the pilot's legal and market role.

## Operator setup

Use a dedicated operations identity. Verify the staff member independently, then run `node scripts/grant-reviewer.mjs invite ops-username --remote` from `chamin/` using the authenticated platform account. Send the generated activation link privately to that person. It expires in one hour and binds to the specified username. Activation creates a separate operations workspace and records the grant. Use `node scripts/grant-reviewer.mjs revoke ops-username --remote` to remove access and sign out the operator. Customer registrations and old reviewer flags do not grant company review privileges.

A customer owner submits registry and authority references from the workspace. Operations then reviews the application, records an applicant-visible decision, and can review a corrected submission. Company approval does not open a port or license a supplier. Verify supplier eligibility separately for each port before activating quotes.

Use `pnpm build` and the global `wrangler deploy --config dist/server/wrangler.json` to release. Apply D1 migrations separately with `wrangler d1 migrations apply DB --remote`. Keep the ignored local environment files private. The Worker requires TEDER_JWT_SECRET. Optional live payments require STRIPE_SECRET_KEY and STRIPE_WEBHOOK_SECRET, with STRIPE_MODE=live, verified supplier accounts, and recorded provider route approval.


## Current release: private global sourcing and execution workspace

Global intake covers gas/LNG, crude/refined/bunkers, coal, electricity from any source, and storage/flexibility. Intake is not route approval. Marketing describes physical PPAs, battery/flexibility tenders, specialty marine fuels and industrial gas/biomethane as outreach examples, not guaranteed execution corridors.

The dedicated `malka` operator handles authority review, clarification, route qualification, reviewed blind briefs, invitations and offer comparison. Suppliers normally submit their own quotes. An optional externally sourced transcription remains ineligible until its supplier confirms it. Company existence, representative identity and authority are separate recorded checks; they are manual, not an automated registry or identity-provider integration. Applicants do not acquire owner permissions just by registering first. One company workspace allows five active named users, including its owner, with reserved invitation capacity, access requests, permission changes and business-conversation reassignment when representatives leave.

Private evidence accepts PDF/JPEG/PNG, up to 1 MiB per file and 48 per collection. Company evidence is restricted to its applicant/owner and operations. DMs are one-to-one: each customer may contact operations, and introduced trading representatives may contact their counterparty. Only named participants can read trade conversations; operations is not a hidden reader. Uploads and message attachments remain private.

Current fee terms must be accepted before quoting and disclosure. Reviewed offers are shortlisted, identities disclosed for due diligence before binding acceptance, and both parties record acceptance of the contract reference and agreed security terms. Requirement revisions invalidate prior sourcing offers and fee acceptance. Delivery instalments support buyer disputes, supplier corrections, provider payment status, refunds, cancellation proposals, completion and independent repeat mandates. Uploaded documents and checked milestones record evidence; they do not independently prove physical delivery.

### Payment integration and activation

The implemented path uses supplier-account Stripe Connect direct charges, bank-transfer funding and a server-calculated 2% application fee. The default supplier-pays case allocates 98% to the supplier and 2% to Teder before provider processing charges. Buyer-pays adds the commission to the covered energy value. Supported integration currencies are USD, GBP, EUR and JPY, subject to provider country/capability/size approval. Teder does not pool trade principal in its platform balance. This does not remove all platform obligations or provider risk.

Payments remain blocked until Pendia LLC supplies its separately configured Teder credentials, the provider has approved the activity and responsibility settings, and operations records approval for the actual product/country/currency/maximum/expiry. Supplier owners then complete hosted provider onboarding. Configure a connected-account event destination at `/api/stripe/webhook` for checkout payment status, payment intents, charges/refunds/disputes and application-fee refunds. Its signing secret must match STRIPE_WEBHOOK_SECRET. Do not set STRIPE_MODE=test in production. No Stripe account or separate testing environment is provisioned by the code.

A checkout is an instruction to pay, not confirmation. Signed, account-matched provider events reconcile the exact amount, currency, charge and fee. Duplicate checkout attempts, events and refunds use durable identifiers. Refunds reverse a proportional application fee; contractual disputes and provider disputes remain distinct. A non-provider fee receipt cannot mark a new mandate paid.

### Release checks

`pnpm exec tsx tests/workflows.test.ts` exercises real API authentication, registration/recovery confirmation, manual clarification and approval, evidence access, support and trade DMs, five-seat/access-request/handover rules, sourcing, confirmed quotes, disclosure, instalment corrections and signed payment/refund events against isolated SQLite. Payment-provider responses are local fixtures, not actual Stripe API verification. `pnpm exec tsx tests/energy.test.ts` protects legacy cases. Run `pnpm exec tsc --noEmit`, `npm run build`, real browser flows, and apply migrations through 0020 before deployment. No successful real-money energy transaction is established by these checks.
