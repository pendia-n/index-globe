# Teder

The global energy workspace at `/workspace/energy` accepts private buyer requirements and available supplier supply for natural gas/LNG, crude/refined products, physical coal, electricity from any generation source, and storage/flexibility. The homepage provides both entry points. Delivery jurisdiction uses the existing full country/territory list. The original bunker and Singapore power records remain accessible.

Customers can submit before company approval. A dedicated operator assigns the case, sets a response deadline, and records route clearance after company approval. Supply availability becomes eligible for same-product, same-country suggestions until its expiry. Suggestions require confirmation of delivery point, quantities, timing and specifications. Buyers or operations invite approved suppliers privately. Each supplier sees its own offers.

A buyer selects a current quote version. Operations records the contract reference, payment-security arrangement and fee terms. Both parties accept before supplier delivery reporting. The buyer reviews delivery, supplier records the invoice, buyer reports payment, supplier confirms receipt, and operations reconciles the separately agreed Teder fee. Exceptions pause progress and require a documented resolution. Recorded external evidence references support these checks.

Run `./node_modules/.bin/tsx tests/energy.test.ts`, `./node_modules/.bin/tsc --noEmit`, and `npm run build`. The regression exercises private quote isolation, stale quotes, role gates, exceptions, delivery and fee reconciliation against all migrations in isolated SQLite. Live anonymous page and API checks are separate from real-party transactions. Energy notices feed the existing authenticated inbox. External email alerts require a configured delivery provider.

Company owners register one workspace per legal entity using its registration jurisdiction and registry identifier. A reviewer checks the registry record, representative and pilot eligibility before enabling requests and offers. Each company has five named seats, including the owner. An unused invitation reserves a seat for 24 hours or until the owner revokes it. Review authority is granted to a trusted account through an authenticated D1 administrative operation after identity verification. There is no public reviewer registration.

The browser signs in using an HttpOnly signed session cookie. Save the generated recovery code during registration. A password reset requires the username and current recovery code, replaces it with a newly saved code and revokes existing sessions. There is no email reset. The JWT signing secret is stored only in the ignored local environment file and a Worker secret. Keep a protected backup of the signing secret. Rotation requires a deliberate migration because it also protects password and recovery verifiers.

The buyer and seller sign and settle outside Teder. A delivery report identifies its source and evidence reference. Suppliers can include half-hour readings for the buyer to compare against the scheduled amount. The buyer can reconcile delivery or open a dispute and review a corrected report. The app does not move funds, schedule a grid transaction, or independently confirm a meter reading. A live deal requires eligible participants, an appropriate route and review of the pilot's legal and market role.

## Operator setup

Use a dedicated operations identity. Verify the staff member independently, then run `node scripts/grant-reviewer.mjs invite ops-username --remote` from `chamin/` using the authenticated platform account. Send the generated activation link privately to that person. It expires in one hour and binds to the specified username. Activation creates a separate operations workspace and records the grant. Use `node scripts/grant-reviewer.mjs revoke ops-username --remote` to remove access and sign out the operator. Customer registrations and old reviewer flags do not grant company review privileges.

A customer owner submits registry and authority references from the workspace. Operations then reviews the application, records an applicant-visible decision, and can review a corrected submission. Company approval does not open a port or license a supplier. Verify supplier eligibility separately for each port before activating quotes.

Use `pnpm build` and the global `wrangler deploy --config dist/server/wrangler.json` to release. Apply D1 migrations separately with `wrangler d1 migrations apply DB --remote`. The production Worker needs only the named JWT secret. Keep the ignored .env private and upload only that key as a Worker secret.
