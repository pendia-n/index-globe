# Index Globe

Index Globe helps an independent electricity generator, a business buyer and a licensed supplier work through a power deal. The first proposed market is Great Britain. Offers can distinguish renewable generation, nuclear and gas-fired electricity. Source labels do not certify environmental claims. The public market shows producer listings and buyer requests from the database.

A buyer can compare demand with generation and see the share that needs remaining supply. In the private workspace, the parties keep the current offer, quote history, supplier review and meter evidence together. The app makes it clear when someone still needs to act. Its purpose is to carry agreed terms into an auditable handoff for a supplier, from an offer to metered evidence.

The grid operator manages physical electricity flow. A licensed supplier must agree the live arrangements and is responsible for official supply, invoices, credits and settlement. An in-app record is a review step, not a market nomination or grid instruction.

## Commercial model

Exploration and private deal drafting are free. A supplier pilot is priced by fixed quote for an agreed workflow and data format. After pilot validation, quote a recurring fee for the supplier's workspace. Index Globe charges for its software and services. The supplier handles electricity payments.
